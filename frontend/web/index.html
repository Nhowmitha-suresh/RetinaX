CLINICAL TRUST & DATA PRIVACY

RetinaX is engineered strictly as a clinical decision support screening aid to assist certified eye care practitioners. Image processing is executed locally on-device without unauthorized cloud data persistence, protecting patient privacy and aligning with HIPAA & GDPR data handling frameworks.

Clinical Classification Reference

DR SEVERITY REFERENCE SCALE

Overview of the five clinical stages according to the International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy Disease Severity Scale.

Level Stage Name Clinical Characteristics Recommended Action
Level 0 NO DR No visible microaneurysms or retinal lesions. Annual routine screening
Level 1 MILD NPDR Microaneurysms only. Follow-up in 6 to 12 months
Level 2 MODERATE NPDR More than microaneurysms but less than severe NPDR. Hard exudates or cotton wool spots. Referral to ophthalmologist within 3 months
Level 3 SEVERE NPDR Intraretinal hemorrhages in 4 quadrants, venous beading in 2+ quadrants, or IRMA in 1+ quadrant. Prompt ophthalmologist consultation within 4 weeks
Level 4 PROLIFERATIVE DR Neovascularization or vitreous/preretinal hemorrhage. High risk of vision loss. Urgent retina specialist evaluation

RETINAX OPERATIONAL USER GUIDE

Clinical Workflow Assistance for Technicians & Clinicians

1. Assistant Mode (Technicians)

Capture retinal fundus images using a fundus camera, desktop upload, webcam, or mobile camera sync. Enter patient parameters, run the ResNet152 AI classifier, download clinical PDF reports, and route reports directly to nearby hospitals/PHCs.

2. Doctor Mode (Ophthalmologists)

Review incoming patient screening queues, analyze Grad-CAM heatmaps, verify 5-class probability distributions, add diagnostic notes, and sign off on reports.

3. Hospital & PHC Data Transmission

Use the "Find Doctors" section to locate real nearby Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and government eye care hospitals. Click "Route Report to Hospital" to dispatch timestamped patient screening records.
